## Evidence-use policy

- Standards, validity theory, and generalizability theory define the measurement obligations.
- Peer-reviewed human–AI experiments motivate behavioral opportunities and counterfactual tests.
- Preprints motivate hypotheses or implementation experiments only.
- No cited paper is treated as direct validation of AOS.
