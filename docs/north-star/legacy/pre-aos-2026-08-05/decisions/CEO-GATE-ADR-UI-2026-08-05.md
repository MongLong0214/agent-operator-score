# CEO Gate — Score UI ADR addendum

- Date: 2026-08-05
- Scope: `ADR-0011` and `ADR-0012`
- Verdict: **PASS**

## Evidence

- The UI remains a local projection of canonical results and cannot become a second scorer.
- SVG/PNG and HTML add no mandatory network, account, telemetry, or hosted storage surface.
- Snapshot, M19 safety, withheld-score states, Opportunity display, privacy allowlisting, accessibility, and prohibited claims fail closed.
- Hosted sharing and automatic posting remain outside v0.1 and require a later decision.

## Authorization

PRD F9 authoring is authorized against ADR-0011/0012 and the unchanged north-star. Product implementation remains blocked until PRD and atomic-ticket addenda pass their own gates.
