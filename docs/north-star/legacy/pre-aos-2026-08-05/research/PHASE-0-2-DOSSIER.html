Adverse evidence first

Construct risk. Task, session, environment, and judge variance may dominate person variance. G1 must be allowed to disprove the individual score.
Judge risk. Position, style, and verbosity bias prevent a model judge from overruling deterministic evidence.
Pack feasibility. The proposed upper bounds total exactly 45 minutes, leaving no demonstrated p90 margin.
Prescription eligibility. A metric needs two authoritative opportunities, which a one-scenario-per-family pack may fail to provide.
Local security. A machine owner can inspect distributed tasks. Initial output is self-improvement-only and never a credential.
